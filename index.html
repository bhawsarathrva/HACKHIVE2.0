$ sudo FAQs;

i am a beginner, can i participate ?

absolutely. we will have mentors and resources available, along with several workshops targeted for beginners. come learn and experience your first hackathon at HackHive.

will you support or reimburse our travel expenses ?

since HackHive is a community-driven hackathon, so unfortunately we will not be able to support you with travel.

i am below 18 years of age, can i participate in the hackathon ?

yes! you can participate in the hackathon but we would need a written consent from your parent/guardian and, you wouldn't be allowed to leave the venue during the hackathon.

what is the maximum team size ? can i participate as a solo hunter ?

we encourage you to make a team of minimum 2 and maximum 4 members. if you are a solo hunter and do not have a team, you can still register and we will help you find one during hackathon. you can join our discord server for some members.

What is HackHive 2.0?

HackHive 2.0 is a community hackathon by SDSF DAVV — a space to learn, network, build and have fun with people who love tech as much as you do.

Who can participate?

Anyone with curiosity for tech — students, professionals, designers and first-time hackers are all welcome.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Participation is completely free, including food, swag and workshops.

What is the team size?

Teams of 2 to 4 members. You can also register solo and find teammates at the venue.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not necessarily. Designers, product thinkers and no-code builders make great hackathon teammates.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, charger, valid ID and a lot of enthusiasm. We handle the rest.

i have more questions, how and where can I reach out ?

you can reach out to us on our discord server in #🆘-ask-for-help channel or email us at hackhive26@gmail.com ! we're always ready to answer your questions.
